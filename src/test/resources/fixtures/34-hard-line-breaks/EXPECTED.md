# 34-hard-line-breaks

Hard line breaks in both syntaxes: two trailing spaces and a backslash.

- `# Address`: equal.
- `Line one` / `Line two`: a hard break (trailing spaces) added: modified; the only word change is the added break, shown as `ins.mdr-ws-break` before the `<br>`.
- `Street 1` / `City`: the trailing-space hard break removed: modified; the left side shows `del.mdr-ws-break`.
- `First` / `Second`: the backslash hard break removed: modified.
- `Alpha` / `Beta`: trailing spaces replaced by a backslash: equal (same render).
